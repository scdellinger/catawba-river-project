## Gauge exploratory

- gauge data runs from 10-01-2001 to 12-31-2025
- there are 8780 days of gauge data
- gauge data covers 25 years and 3 months

#### outliers by year

 - 2002 has the least number of gauge outliers with 2
 - 2018 has the most number of outliers with 64
 - 2020 has the third most outliers with 36
 - 2018 and 2020 were the top two years with discharge outliers, and are both top three with gauge outliers
 - 2024,the year of hurricane Helene, has 11 outlier days, ranking 11th out of 39

 ### outliers by month

 - December and September have the most gauge outliers, both having 45 over the life of the data.
 - When looking at average gauge reading by month of the calendar, the gauge hight variation mimics that discharge variation, with hight being up in winter/spring months, and receding during summer/fall months. Both also show a brief rise in september before falling again in october and november.

 ### Helene comparison with discharge
 - when looking at the changes in discharge and gauge from september 20th to october 30th, I also looked at the multiple of change from one day to the next, 'how does todays mean value compare to yesterdays'.
 - that analysis shows that discharge shows a quick drop in value over 2-3 days, then a more gradual decline to baseline over the next few days. 
 - Gauge shows the same pattern, but the quick drop happens in 1-2 days before the gradual return to baseline
